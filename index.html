What this demonstrates

Only lowercase outlines change. Capitals, numbers, the original Runic forms and character advance widths are retained. Descenders remain below the baseline. Rune heights intentionally vary where the source design does.

What remains for a finished font

  • Optical redraw and weight balancing. This geometric study stretches some curves and compresses ascenders; it is not finished type design.
  • Kerning, spacing, hinting and small-size tests; dot placement and difficult pairs need particular attention.
  • Agree the exact languages and rune coverage. This preview includes printable ASCII and supported characters in the Unicode Runic block; accents and broader Latin coverage are not included.
  • Choose one direction before completing the font, exports and revision rounds.
